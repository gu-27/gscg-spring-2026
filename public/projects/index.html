Spring 2026

Five Client Engagements

Every project culminated in a live presentation to the client — a first for GSCG.

MLB · Seattle, WA

Seattle Mariners

  • Strengthen the fundraising program by clarifying its story
  • Benchmark best practices across MLB organizations
  • Create new in-stadium experiences that attract diverse fans and boost awareness
NWSL · Spokane, WA

Spokane Zephyr

  • Identify and prioritize high-fit sponsor partners
  • Build a targeted set of strategic sponsors and contacts
  • Develop a cohesive sponsorship narrative for the organization
NBA · Portland, OR

Portland Trail Blazers

  • Grow stronger B2B partnerships by identifying industry best practices
  • Clarify the value GSCG brings to business partners
  • Build scalable partnership frameworks for the organization
Higher Ed · Spokane, WA

Gonzaga Institute for Informatics

  • Research, map, and analyze sports analytics programs across the nation
  • Identify trends, gaps, and opportunities in the sports analytics space
Golf · Spokane, WA

Sunshine Club / LPGA Spokane

  • Research sponsorship investment in the small-cap market
  • Identify large sponsors with connections in the Spokane region and Gonzaga
  • Make the case for bringing an LPGA event to Spokane
