The Trips

Portland and Seattle — two transformational trips.

Spring 2026 · Portland, OR

Portland

Seven students spent time in Portland across two visits — a full day at Nike World Headquarters in Beaverton with Gonzaga alumni Andrew and Hayley, and a mentorship session with alumna Torrey. Both experiences pushed students to think harder about who they want to become.

Nike World Headquarters · Beaverton, OR
Torrey · Gonzaga Alumna · Portland, OR
Spring 2026 · Seattle, WA

Seattle

Seven students spent two days in Seattle — going behind the scenes at Lumen Field with the Seattle Seahawks, then presenting a full semester of consulting work directly to the Seattle Mariners front office at T-Mobile Park.
