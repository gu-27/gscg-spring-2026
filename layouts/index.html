Spring 2026

This semester, the Gonzaga Sport Consulting Group did something it has never done before: it delivered live consulting work to five real clients, sent eighteen students to Portland and Seattle for field experiences with world-class organizations, presented its outcomes to university leadership for the first time, and closed the spring with a graduating class of seniors who leave behind something they helped build.

What follows is a record of that semester — built for the clients, advisors, donors, and families who made it possible.
