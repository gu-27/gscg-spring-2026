Student Reflections

Growth is at the heart of everything we do at GSCG. These are the words of our students — unfiltered, honest, and earned.

"My key takeaway was relating everything back to my why. The story I want to tell about my career should not be defined purely by successes or any given title — it should always be the push to do hard things and fail while doing them."
Jake Scalzo
Junior · Finance
"Nike reinforced how important it is to be intentional early in your career — defining your why, building relationships, and proactively setting up informational conversations. Most career paths are nonlinear, and staying open to different opportunities while developing core skills is what separates people."
Dylan Hite
Junior · Business
"My biggest takeaway was the significance of crafting meaningful relationships within the business world. When someone takes their time to invest in you, you can learn from that — and as you grow, do the same for others."

Junior · Business
