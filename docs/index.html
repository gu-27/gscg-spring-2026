Spring 2026

This semester, the Gonzaga Sport Consulting Group did something it has never done before: it delivered live consulting work to five real clients, sent eighteen students to Portland and Seattle for field experiences with world-class organizations, presented its outcomes to university leadership for the first time, and closed the spring with a graduating class of seniors who leave behind something they helped build.

What follows is a record of that semester — built for the clients, advisors, donors, and families who made it possible.

Semester Projects

Five live client engagements — every project culminated in a real presentation to the client.

MLB · Seattle, WA

Seattle Mariners

  • Strengthen the fundraising program by clarifying its story
  • Benchmark best practices across MLB organizations
  • Create new in-stadium experiences that attract diverse fans
NWSL · Spokane, WA

Spokane Zephyr

  • Identify and prioritize high-fit sponsor partners
  • Build a targeted set of strategic sponsors and contacts
  • Develop a cohesive sponsorship narrative
NBA · Portland, OR

Portland Trail Blazers

  • Grow stronger B2B partnerships by identifying best practices
  • Build scalable partnership frameworks for the organization
Higher Ed · Spokane, WA

Gonzaga Institute for Informatics

  • Research and map sports analytics programs across the nation
  • Identify trends, gaps, and opportunities in the space
Golf · Spokane, WA

Sunshine Club / LPGA Spokane

  • Research sponsorship investment in the small-cap market
  • Make the case for bringing an LPGA event to Spokane
