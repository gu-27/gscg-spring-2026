Season Highlights

Trips, case competitions, presentations, and the moments that defined the semester.

The Trips

Portland & Seattle

Eighteen students traveled across two cities — meeting with world-class organizations, presenting real consulting work, and growing in ways that only happen through experience.

Portland, OR

Portland

Seven students visited Nike World Headquarters with Gonzaga alumni Andrew and Hayley, then met with alumna Torrey for a candid session on consulting careers and growth.

Seattle

Case Competition

Competing on the Biggest Stage

GSCG students competed in case competitions this spring, putting analytical skills and strategic thinking to the test under pressure — representing Gonzaga against top programs.

Institutional Presentations

Presenting to the Board & University Administration

For the first time, GSCG presented its work directly to university leadership — sharing the semester's consulting outcomes, trip impacts, and the group's trajectory as a formal student organization.

Organization

Our Associates Structure

This spring GSCG formalized its internal structure with a dedicated Associates tier — creating a clear pathway for members to grow from associate to full consultant, building depth and continuity across semesters.

Technology

Our Use of Claude Code

GSCG leaned into AI-assisted development this semester — using Claude Code to build client deliverables, design this recap site, and produce the thank-you sites for Portland and Seattle supporters. It became a genuine part of how the group works.

Advisory Board

Advisory Board Engagement

The GSCG Advisory Board met several times this semester to strategize next steps for the organization — offering guidance on structure, client selection, and long-term growth. The group also hosted a virtual social, bringing together advisors and students in an informal setting to build relationships across the network.
